Before you place an iTaoBuy order

  1. Confirm the listing and size

    Open the product page and locate the original seller URL. Confirm iTaoBuy accepts that link. For shoes, check foot length in centimeters. For clothing, compare the seller’s garment measurements with an item you already own.

  2. Check warehouse photos

    When QC photos are available, match the color, size label and included accessories to your selected option. If a measurement matters, check whether a ruler photo is available. Review the seller’s return window before approving shipment.

  3. Compare the total cost

    Add domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and applicable taxes to the item price. Check how the shipping line bills bulky packages, especially when keeping shoe boxes.

iTaoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It’s a product index for building a shopping shortlist. This guide lists selected HipoBuyFind products with item IDs, catalog prices and links to the matching product pages.

How do I use a HipoBuyFind listing with iTaoBuy?

Open the listed item, locate the original seller URL and check that iTaoBuy can open it. Compare the product name, variant and seller on both pages before continuing.

Do the prices include international shipping?

Each card shows the catalog item price only. Your final cost depends on seller delivery charges, agent fees, the shipping quote and any applicable taxes or duties.
